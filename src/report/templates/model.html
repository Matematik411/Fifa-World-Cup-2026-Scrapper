Method & uncertainty

Strength: a per-team rating blends de-vigged outright title odds, eloratings.net Elo, public model probabilities (Opta) and FIFA ranking, each z-scored and weighted, then re-standardized.

Per match: where bookmaker 1X2 (+ totals) exist (all first-round group games), we invert the de-vigged market into expected goals directly — the sharpest signal. Elsewhere we map strength to expected goals with a market-calibrated supremacy slope, plus host and altitude adjustments. Expected goals become a Dixon–Coles scoreline matrix (low-score correlation corrected; goals capped at {{ tournament.get('goal_cap', 8) }}).

Bracket: {{ "{:,}".format(n_sims) }} Monte-Carlo simulations of the full 48-team tournament resolve group standings (points → GD → GF), the 8 best third-placed teams, and each knockout tie (90-minute result plus an extra-time/penalty tilt by strength).
