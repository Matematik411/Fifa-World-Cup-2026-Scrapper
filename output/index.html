Method & confidence

One shared forecast model (de-vigged market odds + Elo + public models → Dixon–Coles scorelines → Monte-Carlo of the full 48-team / Round-of-32 bracket) feeds all three games (Nostradamus, GoPicks, FIFA Fantasy). Each recommendation is a single decisive call with a High/Med/Low confidence tag. Predictions are EV-maximizing (not just the most likely score). Fantasy picks are horizon-weighted by advancement.
