Method & uncertainty

Strength: a per-team rating blends de-vigged outright title odds, eloratings.net Elo, public model probabilities (Opta) and FIFA ranking, each z-scored and weighted, then re-standardized.

Per match: where bookmaker 1X2 (+ totals) exist (all first-round group games), we invert the de-vigged market into expected goals directly — the sharpest signal. Elsewhere we map strength to expected goals with a market-calibrated supremacy slope, plus host and altitude adjustments. Expected goals become a Dixon–Coles scoreline matrix (low-score correlation corrected; goals capped at 8).

Bracket: 200,000 Monte-Carlo simulations of the full 48-team tournament resolve group standings (points → GD → GF), the 8 best third-placed teams, and each knockout tie (90-minute result plus an extra-time/penalty tilt by strength).

Calibration: Calibrated beta=0.341 (supremacy slope), host bump=1.09x from 96 market matches; neutral baseline pinned to 2.65 total goals. Team form (U2, post-calibration): Brazil +0.07, France +0.07, Argentina +0.07, Spain +0.06, Colombia +0.06 (weight 0.15, 25 teams w/ WC xG).

Uncertainty: outright title odds are inherently wide; lineups and form move daily. Treat single-team title percentages as point estimates from one model run, not precise truths. Re-run daily to refresh.

Method & confidence

One shared forecast model (de-vigged market odds + Elo + public models → Dixon–Coles scorelines → Monte-Carlo of the full 48-team / Round-of-32 bracket) feeds all three games (Nostradamus, GoPicks, FIFA Fantasy). Each recommendation is a single decisive call with a High/Med/Low confidence tag. Predictions are EV-maximizing (not just the most likely score). Fantasy picks are horizon-weighted by advancement. Re-run daily for fresh data — see the changelog for what moved.
