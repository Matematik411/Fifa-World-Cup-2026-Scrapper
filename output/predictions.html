Score predictions — Nostradamus & GoPicks

One EV-optimal scoreline per match per league — each maximizes expected points under that league's own scoring (not just the most likely score), so the two picks can legitimately differ. Enter each before its kickoff; you can edit until then.

Nostradamus (RTV SLO) scoring per match (90-minute result): exact = 3, correct outcome + one team's goals = 2, correct outcome only = 1, wrong = 0; knockout rounds (R32 → final) double all values.
GoPicks (gopicks.app, Sentora partners league) scoring per match (90-minute result): correct result = 3, exact home goals = 1, exact away goals = 1 — the parts stack (exact score = 5) and are independent, so even a wrong result can collect goal points. No doubling anywhere; leaderboard ties break on total exact goal picks.

Method & confidence

Each recommendation is a single decisive call with a High/Med/Low confidence tag. Predictions are EV-maximizing (not just the most likely score). Fantasy picks are horizon-weighted by advancement.
