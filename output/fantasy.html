Blank rescue (after a match ends)

Distinct from the pre-kickoff fixes above: once a starter's match has ended with him on a blank (≤1 pt, or a late did-not-play you couldn't pre-empt), you can still swap him for a bench player whose match hasn't kicked off — first bench option Antonee Robinson (E 4.0, plays 2026-07-01). You then score only the incoming player, and the replacement must play later than the man coming out (a same-match team-mate is no use). Any manual change — incl. moving the captain — cancels this round's auto-subs, so once you're managing the XI, handle every did-not-play yourself rather than trusting the end-of-round auto-fill.
