How it works: the captain can be changed during a live round only if the old captain's match has ended and the new one's hasn't started. Switching forfeits the old captain's double (his points then count single) — hence the threshold is the next rung's expected points. Any manual change cancels this round's auto-subs, so only act when your remaining starters are confirmed in their line-ups.

Blank rescue (after a match ends)

Distinct from the pre-kickoff fixes above: once a starter's match has ended with him on a blank (≤1 pt, or a late did-not-play you couldn't pre-empt), you can still swap him for a bench player whose match hasn't kicked off — first bench option Lisandro Martínez (E 4.4, plays 2026-07-07). You then score only the incoming player, and the replacement must play later than the man coming out (a same-match team-mate is no use). Any manual change — incl. moving the captain — cancels this round's auto-subs, so once you're managing the XI, handle every did-not-play yourself rather than trusting the end-of-round auto-fill.
